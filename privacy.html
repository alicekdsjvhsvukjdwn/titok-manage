Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

What this app is

A personal tool that displays the statistics of my own TikTok accounts (views, likes, comments and shares per video) on my personal website. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization through TikTok Login Kit, the app reads through the TikTok Display API: basic profile information (display name, avatar), account statistics (followers, likes, number of videos) and the list of the owner's videos with their statistics.

How data is used and stored

This data is used only to be shown on the owner's website. It is stored on Cloudflare (Workers KV) together with the authorization tokens, and a daily snapshot of the statistics is kept to show their evolution over time.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok settings (Security and permissions, then Apps and services). Stored data and tokens can be deleted on request.
